# Third-party notices

The seven skills in this repository were adapted from the methods of the open source projects below. All of them are MIT licensed. The skill text here was rewritten and merged for this project, and each skill file names its sources at the top.

MIT License text requires that the copyright notice and permission notice stay with copies or substantial portions of the software. The notices below are kept for that reason, and the full MIT license text of each project is in its own repository.

## K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills

- Repository: https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 K-Dense Inc.
- Used for ideas in: `literature-review`, `hypothesis-generation`, `experimental-design`, `statistical-power`, `statistical-analysis`, `scientific-writing`, `peer-review`
- What we took: the phased search and screening workflow with recorded queries and counts, the rule that hypotheses are candidates and not findings, the unit-of-analysis and pseudoreplication checks, evidence binding for every claim, and the separation of drafting, evidence checking and approval.
- The authors ask that work materially helped by these skills cite the Scientific Agent Skills library (Kassis et al., 2026, arXiv:2609.00065). Please check the current arXiv record for authors and version before citing.

## Galaxy-Dawn/claude-scholar

- Repository: https://github.com/Galaxy-Dawn/claude-scholar
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Gaorui Zhang
- Used for ideas in: `research-ideation`, `results-analysis`, `paper-self-review`, `citation-verification`, `writing-anti-ai`
- What we took: the Research Question Card and readiness gate, five gap types, the full and read-only audit modes, claim candidates with allowed and forbidden wording, the claim audit entry format, citation matching rules, and the pattern list and scoring idea for removing AI-sounding prose.

## WenyuChiou/research-hub

- Repository: https://github.com/WenyuChiou/research-hub (catalogued in https://github.com/WenyuChiou/ai-research-skills)
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Wen-Yu Chang
- Used for ideas in: `gap-to-topic`, `research-design-helper`, `literature-triage-matrix`
- What we took: the three gates (open, contribution, feasibility) with a verdict and reason per candidate, the dead-end history check with its four statuses, contribution typing with the counter-signal check, the candidate revision record, the five-part Socratic design dialogue (question, mechanism, identifiability, validation, risk) with its prompts, and the idea of comparing papers by method, data, claim and limitation.

## fcakyon/phd-skills

- Repository: https://github.com/fcakyon/phd-skills
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Fatih Cagatay Akyon
- Used for ideas in: `experiment-design`, `paper-check`, `compare`
- What we took: choosing ablations that test hypotheses directly, checking that reported numbers match code and data, and comparing against baselines under the same conditions.
